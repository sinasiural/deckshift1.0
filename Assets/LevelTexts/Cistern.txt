// The Cistern  (MEDIUM, 62x34)  -  built 2026-09-25
//
// A flooded hall crossed by aqueduct piers. SWIMMING COSTS NO SHIFT (PlayerController.EnterWater),
// so the water is a whole route that is free - and that is what this room is about.
//
// THE DRY ROUTE: pier top to pier top, spawn shelf to exit shelf. Six 5-wide jumps = 6 Shift,
//   two crystals on the piers refund two. Fast, and mostly out of the snipers' easy angles.
// THE WET ROUTE: drop off the spawn shelf (free), swim under the piers' ARCHES (free), climb out
//   onto the landing flush with the far surface (free), one rise-4 jump to the exit shelf. One
//   Shift for the whole room. It is SLOW, and slow swimmers are what the spitter, the archer and
//   the two bats are for: the wet route pays in HP what the dry route pays in Shift.
// THE SUNKEN TREASURY: the card chest sits on the pool floor between two piers, with gold and two
//   crystals around it and a spike pair guarding one pile. Only a swimmer can take it.
//
// ⚠️ The designer has said water "does not work great" (2026-09-25) and wants to fix it after
//   judging these rooms. This room is the test bed for that: every water behaviour is on show.
//
// LAW 1: both routes are movement only (swimming is movement, not a card). LAW 2: every pier gap
//   is 5 at the same height; the one rise is 4. LAW 3: nothing on the dry route drops you into
//   anything worse than water, and the water is the other route. LAW 7: spawn (4,10) to exit
//   (58,9) is 54 apart across open water - Phase has nothing to cut through, and there is no
//   cost to skip that swimming doesn't already skip for free. LAW 8: the spawn shelf is empty;
//   the nearest shooter is 31 tiles away.
