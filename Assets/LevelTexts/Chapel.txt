// The Chapel  (EASY, 58x22)  -  built 2026-09-28
//
// A crypt chapel: a flat nave under three vault ribs, west door to east door. The nave costs
// nothing to cross and holds two shamblers and a bat. That is the whole room - for anyone who keeps
// walking.
//
// THE HATCHES: two wooden trapdoors in the nave floor. A trapdoor holds while you walk over it and
//   drops you 0.4s after you stop on it (Trapdoor.cs). So the room's secret is found by STANDING
//   STILL - most likely mid-fight with a shambler - and it drops you into the CRYPT: the room's one
//   chest (Easy allows one), gold, a crystal and two slimes. The way out is the hole in the floor past
//   the exit door: a burial shelf and one jump (2 jumps, 1 crystal back: net 1 Shift for the chest).
//
// It is the gentle version of the Gallows (Medium): the same trapdoor, with nothing but a reward
//   under it. A player who meets the Chapel first learns what a hatch does for free.
//
// LAW 1: the nave is flat; the validator reads the hatches as holes and still reaches the exit by
//   jumping them or through the crypt. LAW 2: every rise is 2 or 4. LAW 3: the crypt holds no hazard.
//   LAW 7: spawn (4,9) to exit (45,9) is 41 apart, and the crypt route is longer, not shorter.
//   LAW 8: the spawn run ends at the first hatch; the nearest shambler is 24 tiles off.
