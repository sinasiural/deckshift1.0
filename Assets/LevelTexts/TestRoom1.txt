//   ~  swimmable water — draw the whole pool; each region fills its bounding box.

//   P  crusher — put it in the cell directly under a ceiling; it drops to the floor below.
//        Needs a lever 'L' nearby (3 tiles short of it; enemies wake at 7, so put the pack within 6).
//   E  elevator   F  updraft fan   w  acid pool   K  wrecking ball
//   $  Shopkeeper    B  Blompo

//   !dress: off | light | normal | heavy                   automatic props + lights (default normal)
