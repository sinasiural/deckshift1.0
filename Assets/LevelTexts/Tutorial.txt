// TUTORIAL - the first room a new player sees. Built by Deckshift > Build Tutorial Room, which
// imports this file AND dresses the result (signs, checkpoints, the kill-gates, the Shift drain).
// Re-run that menu item after editing; do not hand-edit the built prefab, the builder replaces it.
//
// Digits are SIGN ANCHORS ('1'-'9' = signs 1-9, '0' = sign 10): the importer treats them as air,
// the builder puts a chalk sign there (and a checkpoint). Each !signN line is "KEYS | caption";
// KEYS may be empty. A run of '%' is the Shift drain (TutorialShiftDrain), lying on that floor.
//
// TUTORIAL-ONLY EXCEPTION TO LAW 1 (designer, 2026-09-24): the two kill-gates and the tall wall
// require a card. The 2-wide trenches keep each zombie in its own section: MeleeEnemyAI will not
// step off a drop deeper than ~1 tile, but a Fireball flies straight across.
//
// Layout, left to right: 1 walk / 2 jump (hill) / 3 crystal / 4 cast a card (zombie + gate) /
// 5 movement card (8-tall wall) / 6 recall (two zombies + gate) / 7 altar + gate / 8 look ahead /
// drop into 10 the Stagger pit (the drain takes all Shift, Stagger appears, +2 Shift jumps you out) /
// crystals / 9 exit.
//
// Keep sign anchors >= 5 tiles from a gate: an open gate's arch is wider than its one-tile cell and
// draws over the chalk (sign 7 lost its first word that way). Walls above gates are two tiles thick
// so the importer paints them as masonry; one tile thick reads as a thin brick chimney.

!name: Tutorial
// Built before automatic dressing and stepped corners existed (2026-09-25). Keep it exactly as designed.
!dress: off
!chamfer: off
!sign1: A D | Walk. Take your time: nothing in here bites yet.
!sign2: SPACE | Jump. Every jump costs 1 SHIFT, the counter at the top left. Shift does NOT come back on its own.
!sign3: | Grab the crystal. Crystals give Shift back, and so do wells.
!sign4: 1 2 3 4 L-CLICK R-CLICK | Pick a card with 1-4, left-click to cast it, right-click to put it back. That zombie is holding the gate shut.
!sign5: | Too tall to jump. Cards move you too: pick CREATE PLATFORM, left-click beside the wall, climb.
!sign6: R | Hand running dry? RECALL puts your played cards back in your hand. It costs Shift, more each time in the same room.
!sign7: E | Use things. This altar opens the gate, for a price in Shift.
!sign8: CTRL | Hold and aim the mouse to look around. Look down before you drop: something is waiting.
!sign10: | This crack drinks Shift. At zero, a STAGGER card appears in your hand: it buys 2 Shift with your health, and costs more each time. Play it, then jump out.
!sign9: E M ESC | Leave through the arch. In a real run, M shows your route and ESC pauses.
