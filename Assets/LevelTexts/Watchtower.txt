// The Watchtower  (HARD, 40x50)  -  built 2026-09-28
//
// A prison watchtower, climbed from the gatehouse at its foot to the gallery at its top: ten ledges
// on alternating walls, a rise of 4 each. Ten jumps, and three of the ledges you must land on sit
// level with an ARCHER'S NEST cut into the tower wall.
//
// An archer only looses when you are within 1.5 tiles of its height (the Archery Range, Easy, is
// where this is taught). So each nest ledge is a moment: land, and the archer beside you draws.
// Jump on at once and you are out of its line before it fires twice - or turn into the nest and kill
// it, because every nest keeps loot behind its archer: a crystal and gold, the card chest, and at the
// top the relic chest.
//
// Between the nests: a spitter holding one ledge, a Rotbrute holding another (a fight on a 4-wide
// ledge above a long drop), a bat in the open shaft. A fall costs every jump above the ledge you
// land on, which is what makes a Hard room of a climb.
//
// SHIFT: 10 jumps up. 5 crystals, 2 of them behind archers: a clean, greedy climb nets -5; a careful
//   one that skips the nests nets -7. Net-negative for the average player, as Elite should be.
//
// LAW 1: jumping only; the nests are off the line. LAW 2: every rise is 4, same-wall ledges 8 apart.
//   LAW 7: spawn (4,4) to exit (35,43) is 70 apart through solid rock. LAW 8: the gatehouse stands a
//   step above the tower floor (no zombie climbs it) and is roofed by 6 rows of rock, so nest 1's
//   archer, the nearest, cannot see into it.
!name: Watchtower
!tier: hard
!theme: prison
!camera: tight
