// The Pit and the Pendulum  (HARD, 84x26)  -  built 2026-09-28
//
// Five stone pillars over a spiked pit, six 5-wide gaps between them, and over every gap a spiked
// WRECKING BALL on a chain, swinging at exactly the height of a jump. Each gap is one jump - and the
// jump has to be TIMED: wait on the pillar until the ball swings away, then go.
//
// The balls swing in a TRAVELLING WAVE (the importer offsets each by its column), so the gaps open
// one after another down the line; a player who reads the rhythm can cross almost without stopping.
// A hit knocks you out of the jump and into the pit: spikes on the left of every pit, and on the
// right a ledge that climbs out onto the NEXT pillar. So a mistake costs two jumps and some blood,
// never the progress already made.
//
// THE GREED: two crystals hang in the middle of two gaps, at the top of the jump, right in the
// ball's path. The card chest sits on pit 4's ledge: drop onto a 2-wide ledge over spikes for it.
// The relic chest waits on the far landing, past a spitter.
//
// SHIFT: 6 jumps across. 5 crystals, 3 of them only for the brave: a clean greedy run nets +-0, a
//   careful one -4, and every hit another -2. Elite: net-negative unless you are good.
//
// LAW 1: jumping only; the pendulums gate nothing (the validator does not model them - they are
//   cell-centre props, not solid). LAW 2: every gap is 5 at one height; the pit climb is 3 + 4.
//   LAW 3: every pit is escapable by its ledge, and every gap crossable unaided. LAW 7: spawn (4,14)
//   to exit (78,14) is 74 apart. LAW 8: the spawn landing is empty; the nearest shooter is 33 away.

!tier: hard
!theme: crypt
!camera: tight
