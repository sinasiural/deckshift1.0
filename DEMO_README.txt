DECKSHIFT - playtest demo
=========================

Thanks for playing! This is an early demo, so some things are rough. That's what you're here to find.


STARTING THE GAME
-----------------
1. Unzip the whole folder somewhere (the Desktop is fine). Don't run it from inside the zip.
2. Double-click Deckshift.exe.
3. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC". That's because the game isn't signed.
   Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

The first time you press PLAY, the game offers a short tutorial. It's worth it: the game has one big rule you need to know.


THE BIG RULE
------------
Jumping costs SHIFT, and Shift does not come back on its own. It carries over from room to room
for the whole run. Spend it carefully. Cards do most of the other things.


CONTROLS
--------
Move .................. A / D
Jump .................. Space
Pick a card ........... 1 - 9
Play it ............... Left click (aim with the mouse)
Put it back ........... Right click
New hand (Recall) ..... R
Doors, chests, shops .. E
Map ................... M
Relics ................ I
Look ahead ............ Hold Left Ctrl
Pause ................. Esc (the pause menu has a Controls list too)
Save a bug report ..... F8


FOUND A BUG? PRESS F8
---------------------
F8 saves a screenshot and some details about your game to:

    Documents\Deckshift Bug Reports\

Each press makes a new folder. Open report.txt inside it, write a line or two at the top about
what happened, then zip the folder and send it to me. It includes your PC's specs (Windows
version, graphics card) and your current run, nothing else.


WHAT I'D LOVE TO HEAR AFTER YOU PLAY
------------------------------------
1. What was the most fun moment?
2. Where did you get stuck, confused or bored?
3. Did Shift (the jump resource) feel like a cool limit or an annoying one?
4. Which card or relic did you like most, and which one never felt worth it?
5. How far did you get, and would you play another run?

A screenshot of the end-of-run screen (win or lose) is a great way to show me how it went.


Known rough edges: some sounds are temporary, and this demo run is shorter than the full game.
